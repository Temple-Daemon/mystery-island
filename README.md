# 🏝️ MYSTERY ISLAND: THE LOST LIGHTHOUSE 🔦

### 🌊 One island. One mission. Five energy points. Will you make it out alive?

Welcome to **Mystery Island**, a Python-powered text adventure where every decision matters.

After a terrible storm, you wake up stranded on a mysterious island. The lighthouse is dark, the bridge is dangerous, and your only chance of rescue is to restore the lighthouse beacon.

Explore the island, discover hidden objects, manage your energy and find your way to freedom!

---

## 🎮 GAME FEATURES

- 🧭 **Island Exploration** — Discover five unique locations.
- 🎒 **Inventory System** — Collect and keep useful items.
- ⚡ **Energy Management** — Every legal movement costs energy.
- 🧩 **Challenges & Decisions** — Make choices that affect your adventure.
- 🚧 **Locked Pathways** — Find the right items to access certain locations.
- 🏆 **Win or Lose** — Escape the island or run out of energy.
- 🔄 **Replay Option** — Start a fresh adventure whenever you want.
- 🛡️ **Input Validation** — Invalid actions won't crash the game.

## 🎯 YOUR MISSION

Your goal is simple: **restore the lighthouse beacon and escape Mystery Island.**

But survival won't be that easy!

- ❤️ You begin with **5 energy points**.
- 🚶 Every legal movement consumes **1 energy point**.
- 🪢 Find the rope needed to cross the bridge.
- 🏮 Find the lantern needed to light the beacon.
- 🚫 Invalid actions do not consume energy.
- 💀 Run out of energy before completing your mission and the adventure ends.
